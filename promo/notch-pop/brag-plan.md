# ClaudeNotch: NOTCH POP

**Angle:** your notch just got a job. Everything ClaudeNotch does pours out of
the notch.
**Tone:** daylight pop. Saturated colour blobs (hot pink, electric blue,
lime, tangerine, violet), chunky Bricolage Grotesque headlines that punch in
word by word on the eighth notes, glossy black notch cards, confetti, a white
slam on the drop. The opposite of v2's black keynote stage.
**Format:** 1920x1080, 30fps, 33.6 s. Mixkit "Gimme That Groove!" (#872),
112 bpm; every scene starts on a bar.

Every feature named is shipped (v0.39 to v0.42.1). The cards are rebuilt from
the real ones; project names, commands and numbers on them are illustrative,
and the frame says "Illustrative UI" while they are on screen.
