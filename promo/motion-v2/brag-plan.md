# ClaudeNotch: motion piece (v2)

**Angle:** Claude is waiting in a terminal buried under your other windows.
Look up: the answer is in the notch.
**Tone:** Apple-keynote motion design. Black stage, moving coral and indigo
light, film grain, a 3D camera over a Mac display, big tight headlines with
mask-up reveals, real 120fps-to-30fps motion blur.
**Format:** 1920x1080, 30fps, 25.0s. D minor, 120 bpm; every cut on a beat.

## Storyboard

| # | Time | Scene | Motion | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0-3.0 | Hook | Terminal prompt "Do you want to proceed?" gets buried as five windows fly in over it in depth. "Claude is waiting. In a terminal you are not looking at." | Heartbeat kick, clock tick, filtered pad |
| 2 | 3.0-5.5 | Look up | Whip pan; a Mac display swings in, the notch glows coral. "Look up." | Whip, sub boom, riser, a beat of silence |
| 3 | 5.5-9.0 | Approve | Camera dives into the notch, which springs open into the permission card. Cursor clicks Allow, it turns green, the card snaps shut. | Drop: full groove; click, confirm bells |
| 4 | 9.0-12.5 | Guardrails | Card grows into the destructive warning, lines land one by one, press-and-hold fills with Touch ID glyph. | Warning ticks, rising hold tone, bell |
| 5 | 12.5-15.0 | Diff | Card morphs to Edit file; red and green lines slide in. | Pitched ticks per line |
| 6 | 15.0-18.5 | Sessions | Camera pulls back; the card opens into the session list: rows cascade, limit meters fill, spend ticks up. | 16th arp, row bells |
| 7 | 18.5-21.0 | Pet | Panel folds back; the pet climbs out, gets booped, backflips, lands in a sparkle burst. | Half-time, marimba, boing |
| 8 | 21.0-25.0 | Outro | Flash, light streak, icon springs in with a gloss sweep, "ClaudeNotch" with a light pass, tagline, brew command types itself. | Boom, Fadd9 lift, shimmer |

The app UI is rebuilt in HTML from the real cards (layout, colours and copy
matched against demo.mp4, NotchSessionList.swift, PetRig.swift). Session
names, branches and numbers are illustrative UI, not claims. Headline copy is
from the site except "Look up." and "It backflips. Obviously."
